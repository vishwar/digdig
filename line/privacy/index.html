Privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026. This policy describes the Line Mac app. digdig operates it.

Line watches the clipboard for an ID and opens that ID in the app you chose. It does this on your Mac. Line has no account, no advertising, and no server of its own. We do not sell personal information.

1. Clipboard

Line reads text on the Mac clipboard to see whether it contains an ID it recognizes, such as a Linear issue key, a GitHub number, or a customer ID. It looks again when the clipboard changes. The text stays on the Mac. Line does not upload it, store it on a server, or include it in analytics. There is no analytics.

The clipboard can hold anything you copied, including a password or a message. Line uses that text only to look for a matching ID, and only on the device.

After you open an ID, or after the idle time you set, Line drops its own memory of the match. The default is 5 seconds after you open it, and 60 seconds if you never do. That countdown does not change the clipboard. Copy the same text again and Line can match it again.

If you choose Copy for a matched URL, Line writes that URL onto the clipboard. That is the only time Line changes the clipboard.

2. Shortcuts and settings

The shortcuts, the URLs, and the timing you set are stored in a file on the Mac. On the Mac App Store version, that file lives in the app’s private folder. Line does not send those settings anywhere.

The shortcut is registered with macOS. Line does not ask for Accessibility access, and it does not record your keystrokes. It is notified only for the combinations you set.

Deleting the Mac App Store app removes that private folder with it. A copy installed outside the store keeps its settings file at ~/.config/line until you delete the file.

3. Opening a page

When a shortcut or the menu opens an ID, Line asks macOS to open the URL in your browser. Your browser then loads that page. From there, the site you opened handles the visit under its own policy. Line itself does not connect to Linear, GitHub, or any of the other apps.

4. The Mac App Store

If you download Line from the Mac App Store, Apple handles the download. We do not receive your Apple ID, your name, or your payment details. Line has no sign-in.

5. Children

Line is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions go to the support contact on the Line Mac App Store listing. That is the channel we read.
