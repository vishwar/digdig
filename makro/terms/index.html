Calorie goals are split into carbohydrate, protein, and fat using the Atwater general factors: 4 calories per gram of carbohydrate, 4 per gram of protein, and 9 per gram of fat. Meal estimates use typical nutrient values published in USDA FoodData Central. Sources: USDA Agriculture Handbook 74 and FoodData Central.
